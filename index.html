    How the responsibilities fit together

    Research interprets sources and maintains requirements. Separate Operations contexts prepare artifacts within disjoint write areas. Verification receives the full result and sends its complete initial finding first to the project owner. The owner decides further routing and acceptance. Authorized writers record the consequences in maintained project knowledge.

    Operator Knowledge describes working instructions. A session is the current execution context. Project Knowledge persists through linked source, requirement, artifact and decision records. Read the complete model.

    Set up your own project

    Begin with your topic, an exploratory idea or an intended result. The setup conversation helps define a first assignment and the smallest useful operator structure.

    1. Open a new conversation in your AI harness. Use the start instructions below. The conversation proceeds in your language, with one necessary question at a time.
    2. Define the project and choose a workspace. Review the proposed responsibilities, knowledge sources and permissions before creating files.
    3. Try a bounded first assignment. Verify the actual sessions, tools and handovers, then adapt the structure from what you observe.
