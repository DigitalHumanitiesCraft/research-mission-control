Research with AI operators

Keep the work connected to its evidence

Research Mission Control organises assignments around a research question and its knowledge. Give an AI operator a bounded task, examine its result against the sources, and carry checked findings into the documents used by the next assignment.

Try the comparison, then its reuse

One invented inventory note calls a blue cube a game piece. Another calls a blue cube a sample. Matching descriptions alone leave open whether they refer to the same object.

Atlas holds the question, assigned comparison and qualified result. Exhibit uses that result to prepare a public label. Its label task is still assigned, and the wording awaits a human decision.

Open the result and its source above. Then use conversation preparation to discuss what the evidence supports. Copying prepares text for your chosen chat.

Make the next contribution inspectable

A useful assignment names its sources, expected result, permitted changes and checks. The returned artifact keeps its evidence and uncertainty visible. The responsible writer incorporates authorised corrections and examines their consequences for dependent work.

The practical method follows Atlas from its source and assignment through review, corrected knowledge and dependent reuse, then shows the local setup. Formal responsibilities and exchange rules remain available in the specification.

Use it with your own material

Copy the example, run it locally and edit its authored data. The supplied tools rebuild the interface. A separate Markdown catalog connects selected documents to the source reader, while the optional initializer creates role and communication contracts for a new workspace.

Follow the local setup

Compare the same evidence in project folders, linked notes or shared sources. These choices change where the source lives. The question, original text and research result stay the same.
