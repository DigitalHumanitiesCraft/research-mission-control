## Purpose

The criteria are maintained in the orchestration architecture. [Read the criteria](orchestration-architecture.md#scaling).

Choose one working context for a contained action and inspectable result. [Read the criteria](orchestration-architecture.md#direct-work).

## Delegation

Compare the concrete benefit with handoff and integration cost. [Read the criteria](orchestration-architecture.md#delegation).

## Hierarchy

Add a layer only for a defined synthesis or control function. [Read the criteria](orchestration-architecture.md#hierarchy).
