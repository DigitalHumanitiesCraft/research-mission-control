Research Mission Control in practice¶

Research Mission Control connects project knowledge with bounded AI assignments and examined results. The authored case “Traceable project handovers” connects a requirements study with a dependent 90-minute workshop. Its records, requirements matrix, agenda, worksheet and model handover are available in full. They record no actual study, workshop delivery, agent execution, independent review or human acceptance.

Begin with a question and its sources¶

The requirements study asks: “What does a colleague need to resume a project task from a documented handover?” The original records supply the evidence for a bounded analysis. R2 describes a table reported as “cleaned” without a path, revision, explanation or executable next action. R3 requires source support for completion claims and separates independent review from acceptance.

The requirements matrix retains explicit instructions and marks inferences from stated needs. RQ-02 locates the artifact and revision. RQ-03 separates completed changes, source observations and interpretation. RQ-04 bounds the next action, while RQ-05 keeps an unresolved category decision with its designated owner. These requirements are supported within the fictional records. They establish no empirical finding about actual handovers.

Read the original records in the interface, then compare them with the authored synthesis. The source pack is written in English. The interface's English/German language choice changes its labels, while source wording remains available in its original language.

Project folders, linked notes and shared sources offer different places to maintain the same material. Their logical source identities and authored results remain constant. An Obsidian vault is one possible environment; ordinary project files also support the portable method.

Turn project knowledge into an interface¶

A knowledge interface makes selected project knowledge available for concrete investigation tasks. Define what the reader needs to discover, compare or check, identify the source and its limits, then choose the representation and interaction that support that task. In the process walkthrough, selecting a document exposes its earlier and current wording, the responsible writer and the evidence behind the revision. The activity tracks explain the parallel assignments while the open decision retains its owner.

Compare alternative views using the same task, source selection and recorded state. Preserve the selected evidence when changing representations. Examine incorrect, missing and incomplete values as well as the ordinary case. Agreement between an aggregate and a value computed from the same inputs establishes a calculation property. A documented source check needs its own evidence. Keep technical checks, observed interaction, domain interpretation and human acceptance distinct when assessing the result.

Give an operator a bounded assignment¶

Copy the complete source pack as described below. In that new local folder, the following brief creates a result that the supplied package does not already contain:

Read records.md in full in the copied workspace.
Create results/ if needed. Write ONLY results/handover-requirements.md.
Preserve every supplied file.
Derive a requirements table with an ID, requirement and exact source citation
for each row. Distinguish explicit instructions from inferred requirements
and explain each inference beside its supporting record.
Link citations to ../records.md and its exact heading anchor.
Distinguish R1's superseded permission from R5's current material rule.
Keep H-01 open, identify its fictional owner and state its consequence.
Describe the result as analysis of fictional records.
Claim no actual study, workshop, independent review or human acceptance.
Save the first draft before reading requirements.md.
Then compare it with that supplied answer, record supported differences
and make any justified revision within the same result file.

The complete local-exercise contract states the source checks and stopping point. An alternative grouping can be supported by the records even when it differs from the supplied matrix. Copying this brief or a prepared conversation does not execute it. Use the actual tools and authority of the context you choose.

The role defines responsibility, the project supplies context, and the assignment sets the actual result and writing boundary. Operator Knowledge supplies the maintained working instructions and relevant standing context, including how to interact with the human user. It may refer to existing role and skill documents. A session reads the applicable sources and verifies its actual tools and authority; a file does not become a system message by being named or read. A proposed extension needs its corresponding authority. An explicit pause remains effective outside the scope of a new assignment.

Examine the result and its consequences¶

The dependent workshop applies the requirements in a complete authored plan. Its agenda totals 90 minutes, including a short break. Participants draft a handover, inspect a partner's source references, revise one finding and state the next permitted action. Classroom peer review is a learning activity; it does not perform the method's separate Independent Verification or grant acceptance.

Completed authored material What to inspect
Requirements synthesis Each requirement's record, warrant and realization.
Workshop plan Learning intentions, agenda and university/company adaptations.
Participant worksheet Source and revision, state, next action, open decision and checks. Findings remain blank until someone performs them.
Model handover Supplied prepared tables, a weak handover and its completed worked replacement. The receiver's category summary remains an exercise task.

The university case uses a fictional teaching-collection catalogue. Its ambiguous row U4 remains unassigned for the collection lead. The company case uses a fictional support-request queue. Its ambiguous row C4 remains unassigned for the service owner. Both receivers may summarize already assigned rows and must stop before deciding the unresolved category. The full model gives the actual rows, prepared mappings and complete handover.

A source change tests whether that maintained knowledge reaches dependent artifacts. R1 originally permitted anonymized project excerpts. R5 supersedes that permission and prohibits original project material, including anonymized, redacted, paraphrased or otherwise transformed excerpts. The prohibition covers preparation, examples, exercises, worksheets, discussion and take-away material. Only wholly fictional training material is permitted.

The change record preserves the exact before/after wording. Current RQ-06 cites R5. The exercise uses the supplied fictional cases. The worksheet requests case IDs instead of pasted project content. The facilitator stops original-content disclosure, and the model supplies both audience versions. The existing pack already contains these revisions. Its historical implementation brief describes that authored transition and supplies no new task against the public example.

For a later correction in a configured workspace, the handoff contract supplies the route:

  1. Operations submits the bounded result with source revision and performed checks to Verification, and sends Research a notice pointing to it.
  2. Independent Verification checks the actual result and dependent passages against the controlling record without editing them. Its complete finding returns first to the human user.
  3. The human user decides whether and where to route the finding. Operations corrects authorized artifacts. Research maintains the requirement and its source relation in the canonical knowledge and records the adopted consequence in the journal.
  4. Read the corrected requirement, exercise, worksheet and model together. Retain the superseded permission as history and check that no operative instruction invites original material.

The canonical write-back contract defines ownership. Links locate affected work; the responsible writer examines and checks the consequence. A send operation, receipt and processing require distinct evidence. Shared files alone do not wake a session.

H-01 leaves the fictional sponsor's room-format choice open. Mixed-room pairs compare university and company cases during minutes 72–82. Audience-specific pairs inspect a second row in their selected case. Both variants fit the prepared agenda. Final printing and case-sheet allocation await that fictional choice. R5's material prohibition is already settled within the story. Approval before workshop delivery is also unrecorded. Human acceptance remains its own judgement.

python -c "import shutil; shutil.copytree('examples/workshop-example', '../my-handover-study')"

Check every requirement against its cited record, follow the source links and retain H-01's consequence. Responsibilities and further evidence¶

Research develops the question, sources and criteria with the human user. Operations implements authorized artifacts and returns evidence. Independent Verification examines a bounded result in a separate read-only context and returns its complete finding first to the human user, who controls subsequent routing. A contained task may use one context for clarification, implementation and production checks. The role architecture and specification maintain these responsibilities.

A separately configured monitor compares recorded plans and interpretations with original statements and observed actions. It provides a second opinion under its own communication contract and grants no acceptance or authority. Integration defines deployment routes, notification requirements and fallback.

The public interface and all example choices are authored fiction. The private Second Brain pilot uses a flatter operator arrangement and different review-return rules under its recorded deployment. Recorded Context Stream operation, analysis of an earlier demonstration and the project's evidence limits retain their actual observation scopes. This case provides no measured research or teaching benefit. Proposed comparative studies remain unperformed.

The knowledge arrangement draws on Luhmann's account of stable addresses and cross-references in a note collection (1981, pp. 224–227). Obsidian's local Markdown files, internal links and stated principles provide one implementation environment. Ango's How I use Obsidian and File over app describe related personal practice and durable-file commitments. Promptotyping supplies the project-bound knowledge arrangement. These sources explain the rationale without establishing comparative effectiveness. Gas Town provides a related versioned-file approach through installed multi-agent runtime infrastructure; Research Mission Control supplies portable role and verification contracts. No measured performance comparison is implied.
