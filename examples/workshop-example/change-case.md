# Material restriction and dependent revisions

[R5](records.md#r5-replacement-material-rule) changes the scenario's material rule from permission to use anonymized original excerpts to a prohibition on all original project material. The current workshop, worksheet and maintained requirement use only wholly fictional cases. The before/after passages below are authored revision fixtures. They describe a proposed change sequence without claiming an observed instruction, agent correction or human approval.

## Source change

The previous source wording appears in [R1](records.md#r1-original-brief).

> Participants may use anonymized excerpts from their own project records. Remove personal identifiers and confidential details before bringing an excerpt.

The controlling replacement appears in [R5](records.md#r5-replacement-material-rule).

> Original project material is prohibited throughout the workshop, including anonymized, redacted, paraphrased or otherwise transformed excerpts. Use only wholly fictional training material authored for the workshop.

R5 also expressly covers preparation, examples, exercises, worksheets, discussion and take-away material, requires removal of invitations to supply project content, and reserves approval before delivery for the human owner. Restricting names alone would fail this source instruction.
