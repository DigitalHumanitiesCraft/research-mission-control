# Traceable project handovers

Participants prepare a handover that lets a colleague locate the source, resume an authorized task and recognize where a human decision is required. The plan applies the authored [requirements matrix](requirements.md#requirements-matrix). Its teaching suitability remains a design proposition until an actual workshop is examined.

## Audience and prerequisites

The intended participants are university researchers and research-support colleagues, or company project staff who exchange small unfinished tasks. Participants need basic familiarity with tables and project notes. Paper and a pen suffice. A local text editor is optional. No real project material, account, repository or prior experience with an AI tool is required. This follows [R4](records.md#r4-teaching-brief) and [R5](records.md#r5-replacement-material-rule).

## Learning outcomes

By the end of the planned session, participants should be able to produce the following inspectable outputs. These are intended outcomes, without a claim that learning has occurred.

| Outcome | Participant evidence | Requirements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Locate the exact task source and distinguish an observation from a proposed interpretation. | W1 gives the source revision and section, and W2 connects each statement to a row. | RQ-01–RQ-03 |
| Describe a permitted next action with a stopping condition. | W3 names one action and states when the receiver must stop. | RQ-04 |
| Keep an unresolved decision actionable for its owner. | W4 names the role, available choices and the consequence of waiting. | RQ-05 |
| Review and revise a handover against the source. | Completed peer-review form and a revision tied to one finding. | RQ-06–RQ-08 |

## Audience adaptations

| Element | University version | Company version |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fictional project | Catalogue a teaching collection. | Classify a training queue of support requests. |
| Receiver's task | Prepare a summary by object category. | Prepare a summary by routing category. |
| Source | [Case U](model-handover.md#case-u-university), revision `training-v2`. | [Case C](model-handover.md#case-c-company), revision `training-v2`. |
| Uncertainty to retain | A “sample block” may be a teaching object or a material specimen. | An “access request after course transfer” may concern identity access or course administration. |
| Decision owner | Fictional collection lead. | Fictional service owner. |
| Domain discussion | Distinguish recorded description from a cataloguer's interpretation. | Distinguish a request's wording from a routing decision. |
| Evidence standard | Cite the case section and item ID. | Cite the case section and request ID. |

The handover fields and review criteria remain the same. In a mixed-room format, pairs compare the two contexts during minutes 72–82. In an audience-specific format, pairs inspect a second row in their selected case during that slot. [H-01](records.md#r6-pending-human-choice) leaves the format and final sheet allocation with the fictional sponsor.

## Agenda

| Minutes | Duration | Activity and actual output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–8 | 8 min | Read the question and material rule. Participants identify the source IDs on the sheet. |
| 8–18 | 10 min | Inspect the weak handover and identify missing information against R2. Each pair marks two gaps. |
| 18–28 | 10 min | Trace one row of the requirements matrix into the model handover. Each pair distinguishes source wording, interpretation and open decision. |
| 28–40 | 12 min | Read the assigned fictional case and complete W1–W2. |
| 40–45 | 5 min | Break. |
| 45–60 | 15 min | Complete W3–W5 and the draft handover. |
| 60–72 | 12 min | Exchange sheets. The receiver performs the source-based peer review and returns the complete form to its author. |
| 72–82 | 10 min | Revise one finding. In a mixed room, compare the decision boundaries of U and C. In an audience-specific room, inspect a second row in the same case. |
| 82–90 | 8 min | Each pair reads the next permitted action and stopping condition aloud. Complete the exit check and retain the revision record. |

The durations total 90 minutes. The slot variation preserves the same duration and intended outcomes, as required by [R4](records.md#r4-teaching-brief) and [R6](records.md#r6-pending-human-choice).

## Exercise

Use only Case U or Case C, revision `training-v2`, supplied in [the training source](model-handover.md#training-source). Complete the [worksheet](worksheet.md) with the case ID and row references. Prepare the receiver to summarize the rows that already have categories. Preserve the ambiguous row for the designated fictional decision owner. Exchange the handover and inspect it against the same source. Record one finding, revise the handover and retain the changed wording. Original project material, including anonymized, redacted or paraphrased excerpts, is prohibited throughout the exercise and discussion. This instruction implements RQ-01–RQ-08, especially [R5](records.md#r5-replacement-material-rule).

## Facilitator briefing

Supply the requirements matrix, the selected fictional case sheets, the worksheet and the model handover as printed sheets or local files. Mark every sheet “Authored fictional training material.” Confirm the selected teaching format with the human owner before allocating or printing the final case sheets. H-01 remains pending in this example.

Read the material rule before the first activity. Use only the supplied wholly fictional cases. If a participant begins introducing original project content, stop that contribution before it is displayed or copied and redirect the participant to Case U or Case C. Do not collect, reproduce or discuss the original content. This instruction applies [R5](records.md#r5-replacement-material-rule).

During classroom peer review, ask the receiver to locate the row cited in W2 and explain the first action in W3. Mark a missing source or an unsupported completion claim for revision and return the full exercise form to its author. A correct reference alone does not settle the category interpretation, as [R3](records.md#r3-review-instruction) states. This learning activity follows R4. Separate independent Verification under R3 returns its full initial finding to the human owner, who decides routing and acceptance. The model's open classification question stays open. Workshop delivery requires the human owner's approval of the revised material, which is unrecorded here.

## Exit check

Each pair submits a handover, its peer-review form and one before/after revision. The receiver writes the next permitted action in their own words and names the condition that requires a decision. The facilitator can inspect these outputs against the worksheet criteria. No pass rate, learning measurement or acceptance is recorded in this authored plan.
